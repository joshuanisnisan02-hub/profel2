Case Study

Case: GCash — From Remittance to Super App

  • 2004: Started as mobile remittance service by Globe
  • 2017: Relaunched as full e-wallet under Mynt
  • 2020: COVID pandemic drove massive adoption
  • 2026: 94M Filipinos have used GCash; 6M merchants and social sellers on the app
  • IS Infrastructure: Cloud-based, API-driven, AI fraud detection
SCRIPT: "Let's deep dive into GCash. Started in 2004 — originally just mobile remittance for OFWs. 2017, relaunched as full e-wallet. 2020, ang pandemic was their rocket fuel — people NEEDED cashless payments. Growth exploded. By 2026, GCash reports 94 million Filipinos have used the platform, with around 6 million merchants and social sellers on the app. But it's not just payments anymore — GCash has GInvest (investments), GInsure (insurance), GCredit (loans), GSave (savings). It's a SUPER APP. Behind all this? Sophisticated IS — cloud infrastructure, APIs connecting to banks ug merchants, AI for fraud detection (blocks millions of fraudulent transactions daily). As accountants, imagine handling the financial statements of GCash — millions of micro-transactions daily, complex revenue streams. Fascinating."

BIR eFPS Walkthrough

BIR Electronic Filing & Payment (eFPS)

What is eFPS?

The BIR's Electronic Filing and Payment System allows taxpayers to file tax returns and pay taxes online. Mandatory for large taxpayers and top corporations.

Key Forms Filed Electronically

• 1601-C (Withholding Tax)
• 1701Q (Quarterly ITR - Individual)
• 1702Q (Quarterly ITR - Corporate)
• 2550M (Monthly VAT)
• 2550Q (Quarterly VAT)
• 1604-CF (Annual Alphalist)

Career Note: Every accounting firm in the Philippines uses eFPS/eBIR. Familiarity with these systems is a minimum job requirement.

SCRIPT: "Let me walk you through eFPS — Bureau of Internal Revenue's electronic filing system. Every large taxpayer, every top corporation MUST file electronically. Key forms: 1601-C for withholding tax, 1701Q/1702Q for income tax, 2550M/2550Q for VAT. As future accountants, you WILL use this system. When you intern or get your first job, eFPS proficiency is EXPECTED. It's not something they'll teach you on the job — they assume you know it. So this is directly career-relevant. Data Privacy Act

R.A. 10173: Data Privacy Act — Key Points for Accountants

What It Requires

• Identify a lawful basis for processing; consent is only one possible basis
• Transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality
• Apply reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards
• Escalate breaches quickly; notification duties depend on the mandatory-breach criteria
• Appoint Data Protection Officer (DPO)
• Privacy Impact Assessment for new systems

For Accountants Specifically

• Tax returns, government identifiers, and legally defined categories may be sensitive personal information; other financial records remain highly confidential
• Must secure physical AND digital records
• Cannot share client data without consent
• Must have data retention policies
• Penalties: up to ₱5M fine + imprisonment
• Professional liability for data breaches

SCRIPT: "Data Privacy Act — CRITICAL for accountants. When you handle someone's ITR, bank statements, financial records — that's PERSONAL INFORMATION protected by law. Requirements: get consent, use data ONLY for its stated purpose, collect only what's needed, report breaches within 72 hours, appoint a DPO. For accountants specifically: client financial data is sensitive. You must secure both physical (lock cabinets) AND digital (encrypt files) records. Share only when there is lawful basis, proper authority, and appropriate safeguards. Must have retention policies — how long do you keep records? Penalties are SERIOUS — up to 5 million pesos fine PLUS imprisonment. So this is not theoretical — this protects you and your clients. Module 4 will cover this in depth."
