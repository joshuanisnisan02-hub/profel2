Part V

Case Analysis (20 points)

CASE: "DataFish Analytics" is a BPO startup in General Santos providing bookkeeping services to 50 small businesses. They want to build a BI dashboard for their clients showing revenue, expenses, cash position, and receivables aging. Currently they use Google Sheets. They have 5 staff, limited budget (₱15,000/month for tools), and clients with varying data quality.

Questions (4 points each):
a) What BI tool would you recommend and why? Consider budget and skills.
b) Design a simple dashboard layout: list 5 KPIs with chart types and placement.
c) What data quality challenges will they face? How should they address each?
d) Their client asks: "Can we use AI to predict which customers won't pay?" Apply the 5-question AI framework.
e) How should DataFish communicate dashboard findings to non-technical business owners? Provide a sample SCR statement.

SCRIPT: "Case analysis requires application of MULTIPLE frameworks: BI tool evaluation, dashboard design, data quality, AI assessment, and communication. Show structured thinking across all five questions."
Answer Key Highlights

Key answers for reference

  • Case a: Google Looker Studio (free, integrates with Sheets, low learning curve) or Power BI free (more powerful, good for growth). Justify based on budget, skills, integration.
  • Case b: Top: Revenue card, Cash card, AR Overdue card. Middle: Revenue trend line, Expense vs Revenue bar. Bottom: AR aging horizontal bar. Filters: client, period.
  • Case c: Challenges: incomplete client records, inconsistent categories, late data. Address: templates, validation rules, governance, regular review.
  • Case d: Apply 5 questions: suitable? (maybe — pattern-based), risk acceptable? (medium — wrong predictions could harm relationships), verifiable? (compare predictions to actuals), explainable? (must be), governed? (need policy). Recommend: pilot with controls.
  • Case e: SCR: "Your business generated ₱850K revenue this month [S]. However, ₱200K is overdue beyond 60 days and cash will be tight by next month [C]. We recommend calling your top 3 overdue clients this week and offering a 2% early payment discount [R]."
SCRIPT: "These are suggested answers. Accept any well-justified response that demonstrates understanding and application of frameworks. The quality of reasoning matters more than exact wording."
