Analysis framework

Structure your interpretation before writing

SCRIPT: "Before you write, STRUCTURE your thinking. What's the headline? What's the evidence? What's the cause? What's the recommendation?"
Analysis Steps

Seven steps to a professional interpretation

  • 1. Identify the headline: What is the single most important finding?
  • 2. Show the evidence: Which data points support the headline?
  • 3. Provide context: Compare to target, last year, or industry
  • 4. Diagnose cause: WHY is this happening? (variance analysis)
  • 5. Assess risk: What happens if we do nothing?
  • 6. Recommend action: Specific, measurable, time-bound
  • 7. Acknowledge uncertainty: What assumptions or unknowns exist?
SCRIPT: "Follow this sequence. Headline first (cash crisis approaching), evidence second (monthly cash decline, rising costs), context (rates and projections), cause (declining tuna revenue + rising costs + slow collections), risk (insolvency in 2-3 months), recommendation (accelerate collections, reduce costs, renegotiate), uncertainty (export market conditions unknown)."
Chart Selection

Which charts for this scenario?

Revenue trend

Line chart: 3 product lines over 6 months. Shows diverging performance clearly.

Revenue composition

Stacked bar: shows total AND how the mix is changing month to month.

Cash trajectory

Line with forecast: shows current path and projected zero-cash date.

Cost vs Revenue

Dual line: shows the gap widening (margin compression).

A/R aging

Horizontal bar: shows how much is current vs 30/60/90+ days.

KPI cards

Big numbers: Total revenue, net margin %, cash balance, overdue AR.

SCRIPT: "You need minimum 3 charts. I recommend: revenue by product line (line chart), cash trajectory with projection (line), and AR aging (bar). These tell the story: products diverging, cash declining, receivables stuck."
Brief Template

One-page analytics interpretation brief

Structure

1. Executive summary (3-4 sentences)
2. Key findings (3 bullet points + charts)
3. Root cause analysis (why is this happening?)
4. Risk assessment (what if we do nothing?)
5. Recommendations (2-3 specific actions)
6. Data sources and limitations

Quality criteria

• Clear headline finding
• Correct chart choices with labels
• Evidence-based conclusions
• Specific recommendations with owners
• Acknowledged limitations
• Professional formatting and writing

SCRIPT: "One page forces discipline. You cannot ramble. Every sentence must carry weight. Lead with the most important finding. Support with data. Recommend action. Acknowledge what you don't know."

Presentation Guide

2-minute SCR presentation of your findings

  • S (Situation, 15 sec): "GenSan Fresh Foods showed stable total revenue in H1 2026..."
  • C (Complication, 45 sec): "However, three converging problems create urgency: fresh tuna revenue declined 27%, costs rose 8%, and cash dropped from ₱420K to ₱180K with ₱680K stuck in overdue receivables."
  • R (Resolution, 60 sec): "We recommend three immediate actions: (1) aggressive collections on the ₱680K overdue, (2) cost reduction in raw materials procurement, (3) investigate fresh tuna decline — is it market or operations?"
SCRIPT: "Practice this structure. Two minutes, three parts. Don't read your entire brief. Tell the story: context, problem, action. That's what leaders need to hear."
