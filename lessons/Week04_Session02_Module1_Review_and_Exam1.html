☐ Exec summary: clear problem + recommendation?
☐ Criteria: justified weights?
☐ Scores: evidence-based?
☐ Recommendation: specific + actionable?
☐ Writing: concise? Professional?
☐ Sources: cited? Credible?
☐ Format: clean? Easy to scan?

Key Concepts

Rapid Review: Must-Know Concepts

IS Components

People, Hardware, Software, Data, Processes (+Networks)

IS Evolution

Mainframe → PC → Internet → Cloud → AI (2020s)

DT Definition

Fundamental change in business through digital technology (not just buying software)

4 Pillars of DT

Process, Business Model, Domain, Cultural Transformation

DT Failure Rate

70% fail. #1 reason: people resistance

Maturity Stages

Initial → Developing → Defined → Managed → Transformative

Platform vs Pipeline

Platform: facilitates connections. Pipeline: makes/delivers products

Network Effects

More users = more value = more users (flywheel)

Revenue Models

Commission, Subscription, Freemium, Ads, Data, Listing

Cloud Models

IaaS (infrastructure), PaaS (platform), SaaS (software)

GenAI

Creates NEW content (text, images, code). ChatGPT, DALL-E, Copilot

Zero Trust

Never trust, always verify. Every request authenticated. Replaces castle-moat.

DT — fundamental change, not just technology. 4 Pillars — process, business model, domain, cultural. 70% fail because of PEOPLE. Maturity — 5 stages. Platform vs Pipeline — connections vs products. Network effects — flywheel. Revenue models — 6 types. Cloud — IaaS, PaaS, SaaS. GenAI — creates content. Zero Trust — never trust, always verify. These are your KEY CONCEPTS. Exam Format

Comprehensive Exam 1: Format & Guidelines

Exam Structure

• Part I: Multiple Choice (20 items × 1pt = 20pts)
• Part II: True/False with correction (10 items × 2pts = 20pts)
• Part III: Short Answer (5 items × 4pts = 20pts)
• Part IV: Essay/Application (2 items × 10pts = 20pts)
• Part V: Case Analysis (1 item × 20pts = 20pts)

Total: 100 points

Guidelines

• Time: 1 hour 30 minutes
• Closed book, closed notes
• Write legibly, use blue/black pen
• For essays: structure with intro-body-conclusion
• For case analysis: apply frameworks learned
• Read questions carefully before answering
• No erasures in multiple choice

SCRIPT: "Exam format: 5 parts, 100 points total. Multiple Choice — 20 items, tests basic knowledge. True/False WITH CORRECTION — if false, you must explain WHY ug give the correct answer. Short Answer — 5 items, 4 points each, brief but complete answers. Essay — 2 items, 10 points each, need structured answers. Case Analysis — 1 comprehensive case worth 20 points, apply frameworks. Time: 1.5 hours. Closed book. Write legibly. For essays, use structure. For case analysis, apply the evaluation frameworks we learned. Any questions about format?"

Answer Key - Part V (Sample)

Case Analysis: Suggested Answers

a) Maturity Stage 1 (Initial/Ad Hoc) — No formal digital strategy, manual processes, reactive approach. Excel and email are minimum tools, no integration or automation.

b) Top 3 Barriers: 1) Staff resistance (age 45 avg) → Solution: training, gradual rollout, show benefits. 2) Limited budget (₱50K/mo) → Solution: start with free/low-cost tools, prioritize ROI. 3) Digital skills gap → Solution: assign tech champion, provide ongoing training.

c) Recommended Solutions: 1) Cloud accounting (Xero ~₱3,500/mo) for multi-currency exports. 2) Google Workspace (₱500/person/mo × 10 users = ₱5,000) for communication & documents. 3) Simple inventory system (inFlow or Sheets template) for tracking. Total: ~₱15,000/mo — within budget.

d) Roadmap: Month 1-2: Google Workspace (easiest, builds confidence). Month 3-4: Cloud accounting (Xero migration from Excel). Month 5-6: Inventory system + Integration. Logic: start with lowest resistance, build trust, then tackle complex systems.

e) Change Management: Communicate WHY (regulatory compliance, export requirements). Train extensively (dedicated sessions). Assign champions in each department. Celebrate early wins. Maintain parallel systems initially (old + new) for comfort.

SCRIPT: "Case analysis suggested answers. Key: they're at Stage 1 — very basic. Barriers are people, budget, skills. Solutions must be AFFORDABLE ug REALISTIC for a GenSan company. Roadmap starts EASY (Google Workspace) to build confidence. Change management addresses the 45-year-old staff concern directly. Accept any reasonable, well-justified answers that apply our frameworks correctly."
